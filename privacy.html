Privacy Policy

Effective: November 25, 2026

Beary Light ("the app", "we") is a weight-loss companion. We built it so your health data stays with you. This policy explains what the app stores and where.

The short version

  • Everything you enter stays on your phone.
  • We have no account system, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • We never see, sell or share your data, because we never receive it.

What the app stores on your device

To work, the app saves this information locally, inside the app's private storage:

  • Your name (or nickname), height, starting weight and goal weight
  • Your weigh-ins and their dates
  • Progress photos you choose to add
  • Your settings: units, reminder schedule, theme and similar choices

This information is not sent to us or to any third party by the app.

Progress photos

Photos you take or pick for "Before & After" are copied into the app's private folder. They are not added to your photo gallery and are not uploaded anywhere by the app.

Permissions

The app only asks for what a feature needs, when you use it:

  • Camera and photos: only to add progress photos.
  • Notifications: to send weigh-in reminders and similar nudges that you can turn off at any time in Settings. Reminders are scheduled on your device; no notification server is involved.

Backups

  • System backups: Android and iOS may include the app's data in your own Google or iCloud device backup, according to your phone's settings. Those backups are handled by Google or Apple under their privacy policies, not by us.
  • Backup files: if you use "Back up my data", the app creates a file and you choose where to save or send it (for example your files, Google Drive or email). Anyone who has that file can read its contents, so keep it somewhere safe.

Home-screen widget

The Android widget shows your true weight and today's weigh-in status. It reads this from the app's storage on your phone. Anyone who can see your home screen can see the widget, so remove it if you prefer.

Purchases

If the app offers paid features, payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your card or payment details. We will update this policy before adding any service that processes purchase information.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Delete all data removes your weigh-ins, photos and settings from the phone.
  • Uninstalling the app also removes its data from the phone.
  • Backup files you saved yourself are not deleted automatically; delete them wherever you stored them.

Children

Beary Light is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 18, please use it with a parent's or doctor's guidance. The app does not collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its effective date, and describe important changes in the app's release notes.
